Assignment question 03

No smartphones, no LLMs: attendance for 1,000 people across 100 locations

01

Core idea

Voice-first, shared-device attendance: each site keeps a landline or basic phone, a biometric terminal, or a supervisor kiosk. Employees mark attendance with an employee ID plus a spoken confirmation or biometric scan. An LLM reconciles exceptions, flags anomalies, and calls supervisors or employees for clarification.

02

Daily workflow

  1. Employee checks in from the location terminal or calls the IVR number.
  2. System verifies employee ID, location code, shift, and an optional spoken passphrase.
  3. LLM flags missing, duplicate, late, or wrong-location records.
  4. Voice AI calls supervisors for unresolved exceptions before payroll cutoff.
  5. HR reviews a dashboard by location, shift, status, and open cases.
03

Controls

Location-specific phone lines or terminal IDs, rotating spoken passphrases, supervisor approval, a CCTV or paper-register fallback for low-connectivity sites, audit logs, and exception sampling keep proxy attendance out of the system.
